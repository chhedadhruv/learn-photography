---
question: "Do I have to follow the rule of thirds?"
topic: "Composition"
order: 10
---

No. The <GlossaryLink term="rule-of-thirds" /> is a prompt to decide where the subject goes rather
than letting it land wherever the focus point was.

Centring is right for symmetry, for confrontation, and whenever the surroundings add nothing. The
failure is not centring — it is centring without deciding to.
