---
question: "What ISO is too high?"
topic: "Exposure"
order: 10
---

Whichever one your eyes object to, on your camera, at the size you actually look at photographs.
Published figures are less useful than twenty minutes of testing your own body.

The more useful principle: a noisy photograph of something beats a clean photograph of nothing.
When the aperture is wide open and the shutter is as slow as you can hold, raise the
<GlossaryLink term="iso" /> without agonising.
