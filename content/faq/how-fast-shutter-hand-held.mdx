question: "How slow can I hand-hold before things go soft?"
topic: "Sharpness"
order: 20
---

The starting point is the <GlossaryLink term="reciprocal-rule" />: one over the
<GlossaryLink term="focal-length" />. A 50mm lens wants 1/50s or faster, a 200mm lens wants 1/200s.

Stabilisation buys two or three stops beyond that. Technique — elbows in, breathe out, lean on
something — is worth about one more, and costs nothing.
