---
question: "Is it better to underexpose and brighten later?"
topic: "Exposure"
order: 30
---

No. Brightening a dark file amplifies the same noise that raising the ISO would have, usually with
less information to work with.

Protect the highlights, because <GlossaryLink term="clipping" /> at the bright end is permanent —
but do not deliberately underexpose beyond that.
