---
question: "What camera should I buy to learn on?"
topic: "Getting started"
order: 10
---

Almost any camera with manual controls. The three controls behave identically on a twelve-year-old
entry-level body and on the most expensive one made, and it is the controls you are learning.

Spend the money on a lens with a wide maximum aperture instead — a 50mm f/1.8 costs very little
and does more for what your photographs look like than a better body would.
