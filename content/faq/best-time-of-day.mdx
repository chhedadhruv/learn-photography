---
question: "What time of day should I shoot?"
topic: "Lighting"
order: 10
---

The hour after sunrise and the hour before sunset, if you have the choice. Low sun is warm, soft,
and comes from the side, which reveals shape rather than flattening it.

Overcast is the next best thing and far better than it sounds — cloud turns the whole sky into one
enormous soft light. Midday sun is the hardest to work with, and the answer to it is usually shade.
