---
question: "Why does snow come out grey?"
topic: "Exposure"
order: 20
---

Because your camera's meter assumes every scene averages out to
<GlossaryLink term="middle-grey" />, and exposes to make it so. Snow is not mid-grey.

The fix is <GlossaryLink term="exposure-compensation" />, usually +1 to +2 stops. Changing the
metering mode does not help here — when the whole frame is snow, it does not matter how much of it
the camera measures.
