topic: "Getting started"

No. A smaller sensor is not a worse sensor; it captures a smaller part of the image the lens
projects, so the view is narrower.

What it does mean is that advice written for <GlossaryLink term="full-frame" /> needs translating.
A 50mm lens on an APS-C camera frames like a 75mm lens would on full-frame — that is the
<GlossaryLink term="crop-factor" />.
