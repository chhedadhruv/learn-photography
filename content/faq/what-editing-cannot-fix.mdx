---
question: "What can editing not fix?"
topic: "Editing"
order: 20
---

Three things, permanently: <GlossaryLink term="clipping" /> at either end, blur from
<GlossaryLink term="camera-shake" /> or missed focus, and composition.

Sharpening increases edge contrast; it does not add detail that was never recorded. Cropping changes
framing but not the relationships between things, which needed you to move at the time.
