---
question: "Do I need to buy a flash?"
topic: "Lighting"
order: 20
---

Not to start. Most lighting problems are solved by moving the subject relative to a window, or by
bouncing light back into the shadows with a piece of white card.

Buy one when you have exhausted those and can say what you need it for. When you do, bounce it off
a ceiling rather than pointing it at people.
