---
question: "Why are my photos blurry?"
topic: "Sharpness"
order: 10
---

Three different faults look the same and have different cures.

If **everything** is soft, including things that were not moving, it is
<GlossaryLink term="camera-shake" /> — use a faster shutter speed or rest the camera on something.

If only the **subject** is smeared and the background is sharp, it is
<GlossaryLink term="motion-blur" /> — the subject moved, so use a faster shutter.

If something **else** in the frame is perfectly sharp, the focus went to the wrong place. Choose
the focus point yourself rather than letting the camera pick.
