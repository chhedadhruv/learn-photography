---
title: "Focus on the nearest eye"
description: "When depth of field is shallow, which eye you focus on is the difference between a portrait that works and one that does not."
publishedAt: "2026-08-11"
tags: ["portraits", "focus"]
---

At a wide aperture, the band of sharpness in a portrait can be shallower than the distance between
someone's two eyes.

Focus on the one nearest the camera. Viewers look at eyes first and forgive softness almost
everywhere else — but a portrait with the far eye sharp and the near one soft reads as a mistake,
even to people who could not say why.
