---
title: "The cheapest upgrade is a wider aperture"
description: "A 50mm f/1.8 costs less than almost any accessory and changes what your photographs look like more than a new camera body would."
publishedAt: "2026-08-11"
tags: ["gear", "aperture"]
---

Kit zooms are usually f/3.5 at the wide end and f/5.6 at the long end. A basic 50mm prime is f/1.8,
which is between two and three <GlossaryLink term="stop" />s wider.

That buys two things at once: enough extra light to keep shooting after the zoom has run out, and
shallow enough <GlossaryLink term="depth-of-field" /> to separate a subject from its background.

It is almost always the least expensive lens a manufacturer makes, and it will do more for what
your photographs look like than a newer body would.
