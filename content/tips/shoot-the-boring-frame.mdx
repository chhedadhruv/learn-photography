---
title: "Take the boring frame first"
description: "Get one safe photograph before you experiment. It costs nothing and it means you leave with something."
publishedAt: "2026-08-11"
tags: ["technique"]
---

When something is happening, take the obvious, safe, correctly-exposed frame before you start
trying anything.

It costs one press. Then experiment freely — slow the shutter, open the aperture, move — knowing
that if none of it works you still have the photograph.

The habit matters most exactly when the moment will not repeat.
